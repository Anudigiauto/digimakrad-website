# DIGIMAKRAD 🌐

### Digital Marketing Agency Website

Welcome to the official repository for **DIGIMAKRAD**, a modern digital marketing agency website designed to help businesses grow using **data-driven marketing strategies, SEO, paid advertising, AI marketing automation, and analytics**.

This website showcases the services offered by DIGIMAKRAD to businesses across **Canada, USA, and India**.

---

# 🚀 Project Overview

DIGIMAKRAD is a digital marketing agency focused on helping startups, entrepreneurs, and enterprises scale their business through strategic digital marketing.

Services include:

* Search Engine Optimization (SEO)
* Paid Advertising (Google Ads, Facebook Ads, LinkedIn Ads)
* Social Media Marketing
* AI Marketing Automation
* Conversion Rate Optimization
* Data Analytics & Marketing Insights
